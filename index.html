The Problem

Students face rising living costs and mounting waste. The financial and environmental pressure has never been greater.

📈 17% Cost Increase

Average student monthly living costs increased by 17%, up from £924 to £1,078. Financial pressure is at an all-time high.

💔 41% Consider Dropping Out

41% of students considered dropping out due to money worries. 58% report their maintenance loan doesn't cover living costs.

♻️ 691k Tonnes Waste

UK higher education generates 691,091 tonnes of waste annually. UCL alone discards 38 tonnes during move-out periods.

But at the same time, a huge amount of waste happens...

The waste is NOT because students are careless — it's because the university lifestyle forces waste:

Students constantly move accommodations instantaneously

They can't carry large or heavy items home

They can't store items over summer

Many items are required for short-term use only

Leases end abruptly, forcing quick disposal

This is not a student problem. It is a system problem.
